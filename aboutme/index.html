About me

Hi, I'm Aidan.

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of British Columbia. I'm passionate about challenging problems and about finding creative, robust solutions to them. I work from first principles, and I like hard design problems best when I'm tackling them with a team.

You can usually find me designing and building electric race cars with UBC Formula Electric, spending time with friends, or in the machine shop.

How I work

What I bring to a team

01

First principles

I start from the physics, from heat loads and pressure drops to fan curves, before I pick parts or open CAD.

02

CAD as communication

I push for parametric, top-down modelling so that one engineer's changes carry cleanly into the whole assembly.

03

Test, then trust

When vendor data is thin, I test it myself, in the wind tunnel or on the track, and feed the results back into the model.

04

Built to be serviced

I design for the people who install and maintain the part, not just for the drawing.
