About this reflection

My work at StandardAero Helicopters is covered by a non-disclosure agreement, so this page has no designs, drawings or data. It describes the work at a high level and what I took away from it.

01 · Project example

Tail boom frame fitting

My most impactful project was designing a rotorcraft tail boom frame fitting, which cut the cost of a single tail boom rebuild by about $44,000. The original design was dated and unavailable to us, so I used a 3D scanner to rebuild the model, then improved the design to increase our confidence in the boom rebuilds.

I also designed a forming die for variable-profile sheet metal stringers and built an automatically populated SolidWorks inspection template that saves over 100 work hours a year. Separately, I designed an alternative StableLight Autopilot configuration for helicopters with conflicting earlier modifications.
