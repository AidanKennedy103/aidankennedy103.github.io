01 · Project example

Micro-CNC

At DarkVision I designed a micro-CNC for microfabricating custom, extremely high-cost parts. Previously these were machined on a full-sized CNC mill, which took over 40 hours per part with a yield rate of about 50%.

The design used three linear stages, with provision for a fourth in the future. It has a fully custom mechanical assembly and a custom electrical assembly that uses a ClearCore to control the micro-stages. A fully integrated coolant assembly filters and recirculates coolant throughout machining. The machine fits on a 4 ft × 4 ft footprint and runs on 120 V power and shop air, so it's mobile, and more units can be ordered to increase machining capacity.

The three-axis machine completes parts in 8 hours and pays back its design cost after manufacturing just 8 parts. It has already passed that point and remains the main manufacturing method for these parts.
